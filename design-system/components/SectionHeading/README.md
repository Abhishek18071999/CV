# SectionHeading

The section opener: a mono eyebrow with an index number, then a large title that ends in one serif-italic word in primary.

**When to use.** Every top-level section, numbered in order (01, 02 …). The accent word carries the voice — a verb or a payoff: "shipping.", "built.", "toolkit.", "reliable." — always with its full stop.

**Props.** `index` "01" · `eyebrow` "Experience" · `title` the plain part · `accent` the serif word · `children` an optional right-hand slot (a one-line aside or a SearchField), bottom-aligned at md+ · `as` heading tag (default h2).

**Don't.** Put more than one or two words in the accent, or set the accent in the sans.
