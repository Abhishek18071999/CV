# SpotlightCard

The house card: 24px radius, hairline border, glassy card fill, and a soft primary glow that follows the pointer.

**When to use.** Any grouped content — work items, degrees, certifications. Sits on background or on the `card/30` band of the work section.

**Props.** `children` · `padded` (default true: 24px, 32px at sm+) · `as` · `className`, `style`.

**Don't.** Add a coloured left border, a drop shadow, or nest SpotlightCards.

_Hand-written from `artifacts/cv/src/components/cv/primitives.tsx`; framer-motion entrances are omitted (see Motion in the brand book)._
